Regulatory risk assessment for the board

The NIS 2 Directive introduces personal liability of the management. The management body approves the risk-management measures, oversees their implementation and can be held liable for infringements (Article 20(1)). The fine for an essential entity reaches EUR 10,000,000 or 2 % of the total worldwide annual turnover, and the supervisory authority may temporarily prohibit a member of the management from exercising managerial functions (Article 32(5)(b), Article 34(4)).
